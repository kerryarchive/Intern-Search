Cybersecurity Analyst Intern - Summer 2027 (SAMPLE POSTING FOR TESTING)

About the role
Our Security Operations team is looking for an intern to support threat monitoring, vulnerability
assessment, and incident response. You will work alongside analysts to triage alerts in our SIEM
(Splunk), document findings, and help automate repetitive tasks with Python scripting.

Responsibilities
- Monitor security events and assist with incident response and forensic documentation
- Support vulnerability assessment and risk analysis across systems and network infrastructure
- Write Python scripts and SQL queries to automate reporting
- Help review access control and compliance requirements against NIST standards
- Communicate findings to technical and non-technical stakeholders

Qualifications
- Pursuing a degree in Cybersecurity, Computer Information Systems, or related field
- Coursework in security, networks, or systems analysis
- Familiarity with Python, SQL, Linux, and Wireshark
- Understanding of the CIA triad, threat modeling, and cryptography basics
- Strong written communication
